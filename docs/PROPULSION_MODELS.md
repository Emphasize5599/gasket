# EV and hydrogen models — integrated in 0.0.237

## 0.0.238 speed graph

The tile now graphs energy-cost change (left dollar axis) and driving-plus-stop-time change (right minute axis) against modeled average driving speed. Independent axes mean an intersection has **no optimization meaning**. Green marks sampled at/below-limit choices that cost less without adding total time versus the at-limit scenario; it is not a fitted optimum. Infeasible samples remain gaps and cannot be applied. The slider and keyboard/tap targets select discrete evaluated scenarios, not interpolated feasibility.

Default offset and the “no faster than” cap follow the trip's normal speed rule, including leaving roads already at/above that cap at their limit. Above-limit scenarios appear only when the user has enabled the over-limit rule; they are not automatically endorsed or shaded green. The time adjustment adds the same per-mile `1/(limit + offset) - 1/limit` term used by the fossil-fuel controls to the route's baseline time. This is an estimate, not a traffic simulation. Changed timing feeds back into weather, energy use and charging. Graph work uses cached stations/forecast series in background workers, with no price/discovery requests. Signs, minimum speeds, traffic and conditions override any app setting.

## What changed

`Propulsion` is a pure, offline module serialized into planner workers. It reuses selected-route hourly temperatures retained by `RouteWx`, including weather changes during charging. Missing/out-of-range temperatures use **20°C with a visible warning**, never a newly invented forecast. Speed trials reuse existing candidates/weather and make no discovery calls. They compare at/below-limit offsets, plus user-enabled over-limit scenarios, against known posted limits; unknown-limit stretches keep the route's original speed. Google average segment speeds are not a traffic simulator. These are estimated tradeoffs, **not a proven optimal speed or globally optimal stop plan**.

The existing cost optimizer proposes stops. Up to three energy/stop-clock passes update consumption and simulate actual charging, then check arrival energy, reserves and numerical agreement. Failure is explicit, not a feasible result. The original, unadjusted route is retained so repeated replanning does not compound weather adjustments. Cost optimization still uses posted per-energy prices, not a joint nonlinear charging/stop/speed search; taper and auxiliary consumption can change the best real-world plan.

Charging respects vehicle power, station power, explicit sharing fraction if available, SOC target/cap, conversion losses, cabin/12 V loads, and estimated pack cooling. Cooling is inside the inlet budget. DC session limit is four hours; public AC is 24 hours, destination AC up to seven days. Billed energy is separate from stored energy, so purchased/consumed-energy costs reconcile in Stops, Depart and vehicle comparison without altering the posted station tariff. Unknown DC billing basis assumes DC metering and is flagged; only explicitly AC-metered DC stations use the EPFL conversion assumption. Destination duration mode supports partial charge and parking after reaching a cap. Destination previews remain generic, and the final thermal projection can reject an optimistic preview.

## Profiles and evidence

1. **Default EV estimate.** Retains EPA/observed baseline consumption; uses a generic road-load *relative* speed shape, additional cold/hot HVAC load, capacity-scaled thermal response and a normalized generic DC taper. This is not a measured curve for that car. Do not use gasoline ORNL speed-loss percentages. Temperature-specific fast-charge derating is unknown for these cars, not inherited from LFP.
2. **Explicitly confirmed TUM profile.** Garage → Edit vehicle → Energy & charging estimates. The user must confirm the **2020 Model 3 SR+ CATL LFP (~55.4 kWh)**. No model-name or chemistry guessing. Derived from 44 dyno runs, 67 warmup drives and a warm/cold DC session pair for one vehicle. Drive polynomial uses m/s and watts. Pack thermal mass 479 kJ/K, fitted driving UA and heat-generation terms; parked UA 22.2 W/K. DC stored/terminal ratio .933. These are one-car fitted estimates, not fleet validation. Below 19%/above 83% SOC and cold charging below 19°C are extrapolated and flagged. Low-speed consumption falls back to the EPA/observed baseline. Optional heat pump and 3 kW preconditioning are assumptions, not verified vehicle controls. Starting pack temperature defaults to ambient unless entered. Return-time/destination dwell cools the pack; brief stops do not reset it to ambient.
3. **Hydrogen estimate.** Baseline kg/mile is preserved. A relative road-load/fuel-cell curve compares slower speeds, but is **unvalidated** and marked as such. kg/hour is divided by speed to obtain kg/distance; there is no universal cold/hot multiplier. Retail fueling retains 1 kg/min plus three setup minutes, not a claimed SAE J2601 pressure/pre-cooling simulation.

The 45°C cooling threshold, COP 2, generic thermal mass/6-hour soak, generic HVAC curves and efficiency factors are assumptions. The measured warm charging session itself reaches hotter pack temperatures. Negative power is bounded; the app conservatively does not credit net downhill regeneration to range because the existing reserve DP assumes monotone energy burn. It also lacks verified grade/traffic, wind, degradation, queue, occupancy and charger-operational data. Optional final top-up temperature uses the plan's ending pack-temperature estimate and is flagged as approximate. No queue or outage is inferred from a network's maximum kW.
