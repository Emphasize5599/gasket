# Emergency towing cash notice

Checked 2026-10-14. The Advisory notice suggests **$250 as a planning reserve**, not a mandatory balance, quote, national median or guarantee of reaching the nearest repair/tire shop. No reliable national median for that specific service was established.

1. [AAA's April 22, 2025 towing guidance](https://mwg.aaa.com/news/press-release/april-22-2025) describes typical out-of-pocket towing costs of $150–$250, dependent on area and distance. The suggested reserve uses the upper end of that range; it is not a measured median or route-specific estimate.
2. [Leahy's roadside-assistance page](https://cameronstreettowing.com/roadside-assistance-and-lock-out/) explicitly lists cash-only payment. This is one operator's published policy, not evidence that most operators require cash or that all such demands are lawful.
3. [NYC's consumer towing guidance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/consumers/shopping-services-towing.page) says its licensed tow companies must accept credit cards. Payment rights depend on location and service type; the app does not claim a universal cash requirement.

The notice advises confirming the full quote, destination, payment terms and available roadside coverage, retaining a receipt and keeping cash secure. It excludes repairs/replacement tires and cautions that difficult/longer tows and vehicle/trailer requirements can exceed the allowance. It adds no acknowledgement, financial-data collection, lookup or trip-navigation gate. Source buttons live in its in-app explanation, not in release notes.
