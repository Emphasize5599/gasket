# Persistent traffic forecast reuse (312)

Google describes traffic predictions as combining historical patterns and live conditions, with authoritative road restrictions and driver incident reports, including snowstorm/mudslide impacts. It does not promise that web forecasts are weather-independent or immutable. Sources: [Google Maps explanation](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/google-maps-101-how-ai-helps-predict-traffic-and-determine-routes/) and [traffic-model options](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/traffic-opt). These API/model descriptions do not specify the website's exact weather-forecast inputs. Gasket's policy below is an app choice, not a provider freshness guarantee.

## Policy

Google road-piece departure >=24 hours away: reuse up to7days by default; Settings/Connections can choose1-30days. Between1and24hours:6hours. Within1hour (and past queries):10minutes. Apply the CURRENT horizon, so an old future result cannot remain fresh as departure approaches. Keep mismatch/unavailable negative results for only10minutes. HERE retains its prior10minute/6hour policy. Weather/hazard refreshes and vehicle-energy calculations remain independent; they are not stored as traffic facts.

The cache was already in app KV storage, but capped at1024 FIFO slots with all future results expiring in6hours. trafficcache.js now manages up to8192 records with a256MiB UTF-8 payload budget plus small index/tombstone overhead,128-record hot RAM LRU, constant-time key lookup, persisted LRU ordering and reusable disk slots. Evicted payloads are tombstoned. Unknown old sizes are learned lazily, avoiding a startup read of every file. Keep legacy index/slot keys so old exact forecasts survive upgrading. Original observation timestamps determine age; reading a result does not freshen it. Storage failure doesn't make a network forecast fail. Cache clear resets memory/index state; the owning app clears disk as before. Lifecycle/pagehide and work completion flush the short delayed index checkpoint.

## Identity and safeguards

Exact identity still includes the captured dated local minute, route geometry and avoid settings. No reuse simply because another date has the same weekday/time: holidays, roadworks and events can differ. Existing geometry-verified overlap reuse stays bounded to its2.5minute arrival alignment; new observations carry an avoid-settings tag, and old untagged observations do not participate in this broader reuse. Same exact legacy key still works. Direct TrafficFlow.forecast now inherits trip avoid settings consistently with prepare/between. No sharing across provider keys or borrowing incompatible road geometry. Incomplete coverage retains normal-road fallback and its existing warnings. Unknown/corrupt exact records are not a reason to cache failure forever.

Fuel/energy use is recalculated from the current vehicle and weather/climate setup when applying a stored traffic profile; traffic storage is not a cached final itinerary or hazard approval. Newly added logs report cachedPieces, fetchedPieces and sharedPieces alongside cached flag and age.
